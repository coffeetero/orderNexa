Shipping and Billing Addresses

  • The left list is the customer's contact records: each address is named by its role (Billing & Shipping, Billing, Shipping, or Address), then Notes and Contact Persons. + New adds an Address, a Note or a Contact Person.
  • The address box holds the name or attention lines, the street and the city/state/ZIP. Google checks the street and city lines; the name lines are kept as typed. Delivery instructions go in Instructions, inside the same box.
  • Billing Address and Shipping Address are checkboxes but each belongs to one address: ticking it here clears it on the customer's other address. The only Billing address can't be unticked (tick another one instead). One address may be both.
  • Instructions belong to the address. The Shipping address's instructions print at the bottom of the invoice, which serves as the bill of lading.
  • Validated is ticked by Google when it confirms the address, or by staff. Editing the address clears it. Google's correction appears under the address with Accept / Reject; both tick Address Validated.
  • Existing 3-line addresses: the middle line moves to Instructions unless it is a suite, floor or unit ("STE 200", "FL 3"); the moved ones get a list to spot-check.
  • Pickup customers: Billing only. A department with no addresses of its own uses its account's (read-only) until it gets its own.

Contacts and Notes

  • Contacts are the same people Organizer › Contacts shows.
  • Notes: anything staff want to note on the customer ("Call James to come to the door"), plus the old-system notes until they are tidied up.
