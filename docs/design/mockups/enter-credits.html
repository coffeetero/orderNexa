layout Filters as in Enter Orders, the orders list, then the selected order's items. Cancel and Save sit under the grid.
  • Find by Order No. (default), Invoice No. or PO No., and a Number box (letters and digits; the cursor starts here). Enter finds it and opens its customer, date and time with the order selected; several matches (a PO used on more than one day) show a list to click. Blank + Enter moves to the customer search. Production Date and Time stay disabled until a customer is chosen, and Customer, Date and Time are all disabled while a number is in the box (clear it to change them).
  • Filters: Customer uses the Enter Orders customer search, showing account customers only (as in Enter Payments); the list then includes the orders of its departments and sites too, since they share the account's invoice. Production Date and Production Time as in Enter Orders. The list fills as soon as all three are set.
  • Orders: Order No., Dept/Event, Amount, Invoice, Status; about 8 rows show, the rest scroll inside the list. Status is the invoice's: Not invoiced, Draft, Invoiced · open, Paid, Credited $x. Orders not invoiced yet can't be credited; change them in Enter Orders.
  • Items: Item name, Qty, Price, Extended, Credit Qty (typed), Credit Amt. The delivery charge is a line too. Qty can't go over what's left after earlier credits; the database checks it.
