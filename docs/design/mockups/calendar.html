What this screen does
  • Views: Day, Week, Month and Agenda, with Today and ‹ › and the mini month to jump to any date. Week starts Sunday. Day and Week open scrolled to 5 AM for the early shift.
  • Calendars: several per company, one colour each, ticked on or off (remembered per browser). A calendar is either shared with everyone who has Calendar access, or private to its owner. That is how an event is made private: put it on a private calendar.
  • Production runs from Process Run appear read-only (dashed): the production date and the delivery date of each run. Clicking one opens Process Run.
  • Repeat: daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly (same day), yearly, every N, ending never / on a date / after N times. Changing or deleting a repeating event asks: this event, this and following, or all.
  • Search finds events by title, location or notes a year back and a year ahead, shown as an agenda list.
  • Database does the dates: the visible days, today in the company time zone, repeat expansion and time labels all come from database functions.
  • Not in this version: reminders, drag to move, invites/attendees, Google/Apple sync.
