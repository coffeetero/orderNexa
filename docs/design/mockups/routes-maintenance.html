layout New screen. Day and production time in the title row; routes and unassigned on the left (340px, 300px below 1440px), the selected route's stops on the right. Below 1024px the panes stack. As built: the address sits under the ship-to name (its own column from 1700px), customer number gets its own column from 1440px, and the route buttons show icons only below 1600px (labels in tooltips).
  • Routes: code, name, then stop counts for each day of the chosen time (the chosen day highlighted). Inactive routes are greyed. A filter box for tenants with dozens of routes. Edit route opens a dialog: code, name, sort order, default driver (a staff user), start time, notes, active.
  • Stops: stop no., customer number, ship-to name, ship-to address, delivery window from/to and driver notes (edited in place, saved when you leave the box), move up/down buttons and remove.
  • Unassigned: active own-truck ship-to locations (accounts and departments) with no stop on any route for this day and time. A department with no stop of its own follows its account's stop. "Ordering only" (on by default) narrows it to those with an order on this delivery day and time in the last 8 weeks or a standing order.
  • Print shows one route; Print day every route of the day and time, one per page. With a date, that date's changes are applied.
workflow Add with the Enter Orders customer search (Enter adds at the end); a ship-to already on another route that day asks before moving it. Alt+↑/↓ or the arrow buttons move a stop; the database renumbers. Copy day copies to other days and/or times (replace or merge). Date changes: skip a stop or put a ship-to on this route at a stop number for one date. When a production run is processed, each own-truck shipment gets its route and stop number from here (date changes applied), and the Route Delivery Report prints by route order and stop number.
