layout Three panes like Contacts: views and lists on the left, the task list in the middle, the selected task's details on the right (a sheet on iPad portrait and phones).
  • Views: Upcoming (open tasks with a date, grouped Overdue / Today / Tomorrow / This week / Later), Starred, Assigned to me. Each shows its open count; a red dot marks overdue tasks.
  • Lists: everyone gets a private My Tasks that cannot be deleted; more private lists, and lists shared with the company (every staff member with Tasks access sees and edits them).
  • Rows: circle to complete, title, then date (red when overdue, amber today), repeat icon, subtasks done/total, assignee initials, list name in the cross-list views; star on the right. Subtasks indented one level.
  • Completed folds under the open tasks with the completion date; "Delete completed tasks" is in the list menu.
workflow Type in the quick-add box and press Enter: the task goes to the top and the box stays ready for the next one. Details save as you leave each field (as in Google Tasks); no Save button. Drag the handle to reorder, or ↑ ↓ to select and Alt+↑ ↓ to move; Alt+→ / Alt+← indents and outdents; Space completes; S stars; Delete removes with an Undo. Quick add in Starred stars the task; in Assigned to me it assigns it to you. Sort per list: My order, Date, Starred. Completing a repeating task creates the next one in the database (a task done late skips to today or later; its subtasks come back unticked).
